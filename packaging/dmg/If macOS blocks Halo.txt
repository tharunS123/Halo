HALO
Hold F9, speak, let go. Your words appear at the cursor.

INSTALL
  Drag Halo onto Applications, then open Halo from your Applications folder.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
  macOS says it cannot verify Halo and offers only "Done" and "Move to
  Trash". Click Done. Halo is not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID,
  so macOS asks you to allow it once:

    1. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
    2. Scroll down to Security. Next to the message that Halo was blocked,
       click Open Anyway, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
    3. Click Open. Halo's setup guide appears.

  This happens once. Updates open normally and keep your permissions.

NEEDS  A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or newer.
MORE   https://github.com/tharunS123/Halo
