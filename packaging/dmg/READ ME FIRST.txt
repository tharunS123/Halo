HALO
Hold F9, speak, let go. Your words appear at the cursor.

TO INSTALL
  1. Right-click "Install Halo.command" and choose Open, then Open again.
     (A plain double-click is refused because this installer is not signed
     with a paid Apple Developer ID. Right-click > Open is the one-time way
     past that.)
  2. Follow the Terminal window. It installs Homebrew if you lack it, builds
     Halo on your Mac, then opens the Halo setup guide.

WHY AN INSTALLER AND NOT A DRAG-AND-DROP APP
  Halo needs Accessibility and Input Monitoring permission. A downloaded,
  unsigned app is blocked by macOS and cannot hold those grants reliably, so
  Halo is built on your Mac instead. Nothing here is uploaded anywhere.

NEEDS  Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer, about 1 GB free.
MORE   https://github.com/tharunS123/Halo
